Let $n>2m\ge 0$, $t>0$, and $a<b$. Define $[x]_{[a,b]}=\min\{b,\max\{a,x\}\}$ and extend coordinate-wise to $x\in\mathbb{R}^n$.
Then for any $x\in\mathbb{R}^n$, we have the following upper bound on the smooth sensitivity of truncatedtrimmed mean estimator:
\[
S^{t}_{[\mathrm{trim}_m(\cdot)]_{[a,b]}}(x)
\;\le\;
\max\left\{
\frac{x_{(n)}-x_{(1)}}{n-2m},\;
e^{-mt}(b-a)
\right\}.
\]
