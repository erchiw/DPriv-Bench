Let $c:\mathcal{D}\to\mathbb{R}^K$ be the vector of teacher vote counts, and assume neighboring datasets $D\sim D'$ differ in the vote of a single teacher. Fix $\sigma>0$, and define the GNMax (Gaussian noisy argmax) aggregator $\mathcal{M}_\sigma(D):=\arg\max_{j\in[K]}\bigl(c_j(D)+Z_j), \quad Z_j \overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2).$. Then for every order $\lambda\ge 1$, the mechanism $\mathcal{M}_\sigma$ satisfies $(\lambda,\ \lambda/\sigma^2)$-RDP.
