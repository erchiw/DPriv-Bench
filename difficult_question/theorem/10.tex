Let $\mathcal{M}$ satisfy $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP under the replace-one neighbouring relationship. Consider sampling without replacement, where a subset of size $m$ is drawn uniformly at random from a dataset of size $n$. Then the resulting mechanism satisfies $\Bigl(\log\!\bigl(1+\tfrac{m}{n}(e^{\varepsilon}-1)\bigr),\; \tfrac{m}{n}\,\delta\Bigr)$-DP.
