Fix $\epsilon > 0$ and $\delta \in \left(0,\frac{1}{e}\right)$. Fix $B>0$. Fix a positive integer $r$ and let $w$ be such that $w^2 \;=\; B^2\left(1 + \frac{1 + \frac{\epsilon}{\ln(4/\delta)}}{\epsilon}\left(2\sqrt{2r\ln\!\left(\frac{4}{\delta}\right)} + 2\ln\!\left(\frac{4}{\delta}\right)\right)\right)$ Let $A$ be a $(n\times d)$-matrix with $d<r$ and where each row of $A$ has bounded $L_2$-norm of $B$. Given that $\sigma_{\min}(A)\ge w$, the algorithm that picks a $(r\times n)$-matrix $R$ whose entries are iid samples from a normal distribution $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$ and publishes $R\cdot A$ is $(\epsilon,\delta)$-differentially private.
