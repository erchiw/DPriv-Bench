Let $\mathcal{T}$ be the (possibly randomized) \emph{test} stage of PTR and let $\mathcal{R}$ be the \emph{release} stage conditioned on passing. Assume: (i) $\mathcal{T}$ is $(\varepsilon_1,\delta_1)$-DP, and (ii) for every possible transcript/outcome of $\mathcal{T}$, the conditional release mechanism $\mathcal{R}$ is $(\varepsilon_2,\delta_2)$-DP. Then the overall PTR mechanism (outputting either $\perp$ or a released estimate) is $(\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2,\;\delta_1+\delta_2)-DP$
