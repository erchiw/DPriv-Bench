Let $M : \mathcal{X}^n \to \mathcal{Y}$ be a randomized algorithm.Let $\alpha \in (1,\infty)$ and $\varepsilon \ge 0$. Suppose $D_\alpha\!\left(M(x)\,\|\,M(x')\right) \le \tau$ for all $x,x' \in \mathcal{X}^n$ differing in a single entry. Here $D_\alpha(\cdot\|\cdot)$ denotes the Rényi divergence of order $\alpha$. Then $M$ is $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-differentially private for $\delta= \frac{e^{(\alpha-1)(\tau-\varepsilon)}}{\alpha-1}\left(1 - \frac{1}{\alpha}\right)^{\!\alpha}\;\le\;\frac{e^{(\alpha-1)(\tau-\varepsilon)- 1} }{\alpha-1}$
