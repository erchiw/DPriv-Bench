Let $D = \biguplus_{j=1}^m D_j$ be a partition into pairwise disjoint subsets. Assume each $\mathcal{M}_j$ is $(\varepsilon_j,\delta_j)$-differentially private with respect to the same neighboring relation on inputs restricted to $D_j$. Let $\mathcal{M}(D) :=(\mathcal{M}_1(D_1),\ldots,\mathcal{M}_m(D_m))$. Then $\mathcal{M}$ is $(\max_{j \in [m]}\varepsilon_j,\ \max_{j \in [m]}\delta_j)$-differentially private on $D$.
