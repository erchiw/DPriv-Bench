Let $\mathcal{M}$ be a randomized algorithm with $(\mu_1,\varepsilon_1)$-RDP and $(\mu_2,\varepsilon_2)$-RDP guarantees, and suppose that there exists a likely outcome $i^\ast$ given a dataset $D$ and a bound $\tilde q \le 1$ such that $\tilde q \ge \Pr[\mathcal{M}(D) \neq i^\ast]$. Additionally suppose that $\lambda \le \mu_1$ and $\tilde q \le e^{(\mu_2-1)\varepsilon_2} \left(\frac{\mu_1}{\mu_1-1}\cdot\frac{\mu_2}{\mu_2-1}\right)^{\mu_2}.$ Then, for any neighboring dataset $D'$ of $D$, we have $D_\lambda\!\left(\mathcal{M}(D)\,\|\,\mathcal{M}(D')\right) \;\le\; \frac{1}{\lambda - 1}\log\Bigg(&(1-\tilde q)\left[\frac{1-\tilde q}{1 - \bigl(\tilde q e^{\varepsilon_2}\bigr)^{\frac{\mu_2-1}{\mu_2}}}\right]^{\lambda-1}+\tilde q\left[\frac{e^{\varepsilon_1}}{\tilde q^{\frac{1}{\mu_1 - 1}}}\right]^{\lambda-1}\Bigg).$
